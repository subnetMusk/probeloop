Junior Frontend Developer
Lumen Ledger (synthetic company) - remote within the EU or hybrid from our Lisbon office

About us
Lumen Ledger makes invoicing and expense software for freelancers and small studios. Around
20,000 customers use our web app every month, and our frontend team of five ships to production
several times a week.

What you'll do
- Build and maintain screens of the invoicing app, paired with a senior developer.
- Turn designs from our product designer into accessible, responsive components.
- Fix bugs reported by customers and by the support team.
- Take part in code reviews, pairing sessions and our fortnightly demo.

What we look for
- Solid foundations in HTML, CSS and modern JavaScript.
- Hands-on experience with React or Vue, from a job, a course or personal projects.
- Basic accessibility knowledge (WCAG).
- Familiarity with Git and pull requests.
- A degree in computer science or equivalent practical experience.
- Good written English: most of our communication is asynchronous.

Nice to have
- TypeScript.
- Testing with Vitest or Jest.
- Interest in design and user research.

What we offer
A structured mentoring plan for your first six months, a learning budget of EUR 1,000 a year,
a laptop of your choice and 25 days of holiday.

Send your CV and a link to your projects, if you have some, to jobs@example.test.
