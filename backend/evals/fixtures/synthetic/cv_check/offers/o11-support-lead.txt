Customer Support Team Lead
Brightdesk Software S.r.l. (synthetic company) - Turin office, hybrid

Brightdesk makes booking and scheduling software for clinics, gyms and beauty salons. We have
9,000 business customers in Italy, Spain and the UK, and our support team handles around 4,000
conversations a month by chat, email and phone.

The role
You will lead our Italian-speaking support team of eight agents and work with the Head of
Customer Experience on processes, tooling and hiring. You will run weekly one-to-ones, plan the
rota, handle escalations from key accounts and turn recurring issues into feedback for the
product team.

Requirements
- 3+ years leading a support team of 5 or more people.
- Experience with a ticketing tool such as Zendesk or Freshdesk.
- Fluent English and Italian, spoken and written.
- Experience defining KPIs and SLAs and reporting on them.
- Calm, structured communication with customers and colleagues under pressure.

Preferred
- Experience in the SaaS industry.
- Knowledge of Spanish.
- Familiarity with help-centre content and chatbot flows.

What we offer
A permanent contract, a salary of EUR 38,000 - 44,000 plus a quarterly bonus, three days a
week from home, private health insurance and a yearly team trip.
