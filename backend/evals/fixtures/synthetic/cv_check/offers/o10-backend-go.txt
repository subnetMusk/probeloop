Senior Backend Engineer (Go/Java) - Payments Platform
Fintrail Systems S.r.l. (synthetic company) - Milan, hybrid or remote within Italy

Fintrail Systems processes card and instant payments for about 2,000 online merchants in
Southern Europe. The platform team owns the services that authorise, settle and reconcile
transactions, with peaks of 1,500 requests per second during sales seasons.

What you will work on
- Designing and evolving the authorisation and settlement services.
- Improving reliability: SLOs, alerting, incident reviews and capacity planning.
- Mentoring mid-level engineers and reviewing designs across teams.
- Working with compliance on audit trails and data retention.

Must have
- 5+ years of backend development.
- Go or Java in production.
- Experience running services on Kubernetes in production.
- Solid knowledge of PostgreSQL or MySQL.
- Good spoken and written English: the engineering team works in English.

Nice to have
- Experience with Kafka.
- On-call experience for production systems.
- A background in payments or banking.

What we offer
A gross annual salary of EUR 60,000 - 75,000, stock options, EUR 2,000 a year for training and
conferences, and a paid on-call rotation shared by eight engineers.
