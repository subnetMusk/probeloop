Registered Nurse - Acute Medical Unit
Harbourside Community Hospital (synthetic company), Bristol - full time, 37.5 hours per week

Harbourside Community Hospital is an independent 180-bed hospital in the centre of Bristol. Our
28-bed Acute Medical Unit admits adults directly from the emergency department and from GP
referrals, and most patients stay with us for less than 72 hours. We are looking for a
registered nurse who enjoys a fast-paced ward, stays calm when a patient deteriorates and wants
to keep developing clinically. You will assess and monitor acutely unwell patients, give
medicines and IV therapy, plan discharges with doctors, therapists and social workers, and
support students and newly qualified colleagues on the unit.

The unit is busy and the team is close-knit. You must hold an active nursing registration,
and you will need at least 2 years in acute care, gained in a hospital setting since you
qualified. BLS certification is essential and must be valid on your start date; we fund ILS
training during your first year. ICU experience would be a plus, as the unit often receives
patients stepping down from critical care. Shifts are 12.5 hours on a rotating rota, and
ideally you have worked night shifts before, because everyone on the team covers nights.

In return we offer a salary of £31,000 to £37,000 depending on experience, 33 days of annual
leave plus bank holidays, a funded post-registration course after your first year and free
parking on site. Informal visits to the unit are welcome: write to the ward manager at
amu.recruitment@example.test. Closing date: 4 December 2026. Interviews will be held on site
the following week.
