Data Analyst (mid-level)
Northgate Home & Garden B.V. (synthetic company) - Amsterdam, hybrid (3 days in the office)

Northgate runs 140 home and garden stores across the Benelux and an online shop that grew by
30% last year. Our analytics team of seven works with Finance, Marketing and Store Operations
and is the single source of numbers for the business.

In this role you will
- Own the weekly trading pack used by the commercial directors.
- Write and maintain dbt models for the finance and marketing data marts.
- Analyse A/B tests on the online shop and explain the results to non-technical colleagues.
- Forecast seasonal demand together with the supply chain planners.
- Present monthly insights to the leadership team.

Requirements
- At least 3 years writing SQL for reporting in a commercial environment.
- Experience building dashboards in Power BI or Tableau.
- Comfortable cleaning and automating data preparation, ideally with some Python.
- A structured way of working and strong attention to detail.
- English C1: it is our working language. Dutch is not needed.

Bonus points
- Retail or e-commerce background.
- Statistics for experiments (confidence intervals, sample sizes).
- Experience with dbt or a similar transformation tool.

What we offer
A salary of EUR 52,000 - 62,000, 8% holiday allowance, a yearly training budget and every other
Friday afternoon reserved for learning.
